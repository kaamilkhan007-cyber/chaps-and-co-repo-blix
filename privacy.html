Privacy & cookies

This page explains what information the Chaps & Co Barbers website uses and why, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Bookings

Online bookings are made through Fresha, our booking provider. When you choose a service on this website, you continue to Fresha's booking page, where you pick your professional, date and time and enter your details. Fresha's own privacy policy applies to the information you give them.

This website does not collect or store your booking details. If you contact us by phone or WhatsApp, we use what you send only to help with your appointment, and we don't sell or share it with anyone else.

Cookies and browser storage

  • Essential: we save your cookie choice in your browser so we don't ask again. It never leaves your device.
  • Google Maps (optional): the map is only loaded if you accept or press "Show map". Google may then set its own cookies. See Google's privacy policy for details.
  • Fonts: our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your browser's IP address to deliver them.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the home page.

Your rights

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Contact us on 071 159 1026 or visit us on Jewel Beetle Street, Raslouw, City of Tshwane, 0157.
