Good to know

Questions

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Both work. Booking guarantees your time, and walk-ins are welcome whenever a chair is free.

How does online booking work?

Tap "Book now", choose your service and barber, and pick from the times that are still free. Your appointment is confirmed straight away with a booking reference.

How do I pay?

We accept cash, credit cards and debit cards.

How do I change or cancel a booking?

Call or WhatsApp us on 084 217 7323 as early as you can and quote your booking reference.

Do you offer a pensioner's price?

Yes. Gents aged 65 and over get a haircut for R100.

Do you cut women's hair?

Yes, we offer women's haircuts. Allow about 1 hour 20 minutes.
