Privacy & cookies

This page explains what information the Chaps & Co Barbers website uses and why, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Bookings

When you book online, we store your name, phone number, any note you add, and your appointment details (service, professional, date and time). They are kept on our booking system, hosted by our website provider Netlify, and only our staff can see them, using a password.

We use these details only to manage your appointment and to contact you about it. We don't sell or share them with anyone else. To protect the booking system from abuse, we also keep a short-lived, scrambled (hashed) version of your internet address, never the address itself.

Cookies and browser storage

  • Essential: we save your cookie choice in your browser so we don't ask again. It never leaves your device.
  • Google Maps (optional): the map is only loaded if you accept or press "Show map". Google may then set its own cookies. See Google's privacy policy for details.
  • Fonts: our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your browser's IP address to deliver them.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the home page.
